About Me

Analytical problem solver blending Economics rigor with full-stack software testing principles.

I am a Software Quality Assurance Engineer with an academic foundation in Economics (National University of Bangladesh) and specialized training in Full Stack SQA at IT Training BD. My background in economics cultivates a sharp analytical instinct for recognizing statistical edge cases, boundary conditions, and user decision psychology that standard testing paths often overlook.

Whether authoring IEEE 829-compliant test plans, structuring card-style test cases in spreadsheets, tracking defect lifecycles in Jira, designing Page Object Model frameworks in Python + Playwright, or executing automated daily test pipelines via GitHub Actions, my mission is simple: eliminate production defects before they ever impact your users.

I also hold foundational certifications in Certified Ethical Hacking (CEH), Cybersecurity (Fortinet), and Networking (MikroTik MTCNA), providing me with deep cross-functional awareness when investigating network bottlenecks and application vulnerabilities.
